# SpotFolk — how to work on it

## The value proposition (the measure of everything) — pivot of 16 Nov 2026

**SpotFolk is first of all a map that tells Fabio's story: the places behind his photos.**

Why: the data showed that people come for Fabio — his photos (gallery), his method (Field Notes guides) — while a community map
starts empty and single tools (weather, webcams, «ask who's there») already exist elsewhere. What nobody else has is Fabio:
his photos, the story of each place and of that evening, how he shot it, when to go.

For each of Fabio's places, the map should give:
- the photo (with the licence, discreetly: no extra banners or pushes);
- the story of the place and of that shot;
- how it was shot, linked to the right guide;
- when to go and what to expect;
- a way to ask Fabio.

The folk, the place kinds, Place pulse, Photo pulse, Folk Atlas and the tools stay as they are (nothing is removed),
but they are secondary: extra voices on places that already have a soul, not the engine. No new work on them unless the owner asks.
Visitors must not be disturbed: no automatic banners or prompts (removed on 16 Nov 2026: «what's new», feedback, install the app).

## Before building any new feature: a preliminary check

When the owner asks for a new SpotFolk feature, **before writing any code** give a short check (in Italian, a few lines):

1. **Does it make the map tell Fabio's places better, or lead people from a place to his photos, guides, licences or to him?**
2. **Verdict:** ✅ tells Fabio's story · ⚠️ neutral tool (useful only if tied to Fabio's places) · ❌ takes us away (or disturbs visitors).
3. **If ⚠️ or ❌:** how to tie it to Fabio's places (his photo, his story, «how I shot it», «ask Fabio»), or a better alternative.

Then proceed if it's ✅, or if the owner confirms after the check.
