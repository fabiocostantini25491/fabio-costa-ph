# SpotFolk — how to work on it

## The value proposition (the measure of everything)

**Travel with a friend in every place.** · «Com'è adesso, lo sa chi c'è stato.»

SpotFolk connects you with the people who really know where you're going: who's been, who lives there, who's there now.
Real, fresh information with a name behind it — and someone to ask. Today you ask, tomorrow you help.

What makes it different, all together:
- every piece of information has a face: a folk with a name and a way of travelling, whom you can trust;
- it is fresh, because it comes from who is there;
- you can ask, and someone answers;
- mutual help: the folk leave something for whoever comes next.

The tools (map, Place pulse, Photo pulse, Folk Atlas, weather, forecasts) are the means, not the value:
they matter when they lead to a folk, or when they make a folk leave something for the others.

## Before building any new feature: a preliminary check

When the owner asks for a new feature, **before writing any code** give a short check (in Italian, a few lines):

1. **Does it bring folk closer to each other, or make a folk leave something useful for whoever comes next?**
   (interaction, mutual help, knowing who gives the information, having a folk who can help while travelling or planning)
2. **Verdict:** ✅ brings us closer · ⚠️ neutral tool (useful only if tied to the folk) · ❌ takes us away.
3. **If ⚠️ or ❌:** how to tie it to the folk (e.g. «ask a folk», the author's face, a «how is it now» left for others), or a better alternative.

Then proceed if it's ✅, or if the owner confirms after the check.
