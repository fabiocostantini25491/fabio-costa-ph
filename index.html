How it works

04

Tools

Free tools to plan a shoot, wherever you are or wherever you're going.

  • Sun & Moon — search a place or tap Near me, pick a date: sunrise and sunset, golden and blue hour, twilight, moon phase and the Milky Way core.
  • Planner — a 3D map with the real terrain: see where the sun, the moon and the Milky Way rise and set behind the mountains. Drag to rotate, move the time and follow them across the sky.
  • Analyze your shot — load a test shot and get a shooting plan: exposure, maximum shutter speed for sharp stars, bracketing and stacking. The photo never leaves your device.
05

Spots

A satellite map of 7,500+ photo spots and points of interest, plus places to eat and sleep, workshops and trails shared by the community.

Visitors and members

  • Everyone sees a free preview: one spot every ~15 km, in an approximate position.
  • Members see every spot with its exact location, photos, reviews, real prices and live alerts — and can add their own.
  • Joining is free: go to fabiocostaph.com/#join, enter your name, email and a password. I approve every request personally, usually within a day.

Finding places

  • Search a spot or a town, or tap Near me and choose the radius (1–50 km).
  • Scan this area adds what OpenStreetMap knows around you: viewpoints, huts, parking, water, protected areas.
  • Filter by category, show or hide places with alerts, or only Support local.

Adding and updating

  • Add a place: only category, name and coordinates are required. Photos, description and community notes are optional.
  • Workshops: add dates, places in total, booked so far, price, a link and any special offer. All upcoming workshops are listed under the map. Update the bookings any time from the workshop's card. It disappears from the map on the day it starts.
  • Trekking: put the pin on the starting point of the trail, so treks are easy to tell apart from viewpoints. Add difficulty (T, E, EE, EEA), time, length, elevation gain and where to park: the average slope is worked out for you.
  • On any place you can add photos, write a review (with what you paid for food and stays) or report a problem.

Categories

  • Photo spots
  • Food & drink
  • Places to stay
  • Workshops
  • Trekking

Signs on the map

  • Red dot — live alert from members: closed, works or landslide, event, very crowded, danger.
  • Green ring — Support local: villages and small businesses that live on visitors.
  • Glowing leaf — new in Support local (first month).
  • Glowing icon — new place or workshop (first 7 days). New and special places stay visible even when you zoom out.
  • %Glowing with % — workshop with a special offer.
  • Plane — workshop almost full: fewer than 3 places left.
  • Dashed border — Fragile place: leave no trace, no geotags.

Many spots are places I've saved but not visited myself: always check access, rules and conditions before you go. Read the full notes below the map.

Privacy

What personal data this site uses, why, and what you can do about it. In short: no advertising, no tracking cookies, nothing sold to anyone.

Who is responsible

The controller of your data is Fabio Costantini (Fabio Costa Ph), Italy. Contact: fabio.costantini25491@gmail.com.

If you just visit the site

  • Statistics — visits are counted with Umami, which uses no cookies and stores no personal data: only anonymous totals (pages viewed, country, device type).
  • Hosting and technical services — the site and the downloadable PDFs (magazine issues, guides, maps) are served by GitHub (GitHub Pages and GitHub releases); fonts come from Google Fonts; maps, satellite images and the 3D globe from Esri, NASA, OpenStreetMap and the jsDelivr and cdnjs libraries; weather from Open-Meteo; place search and place names from Photon (Komoot) and OpenStreetMap Nominatim. To deliver their content these providers receive your IP address, as with any website.
  • Your location — used only when you tap “Near me” or “Use my position”, processed in your browser and never stored by me, unless you choose to save a place, a trip or a Pass it on item at that position.
  • Analyze your shot — the photo you load is analysed in your browser and never uploaded.
  • Public map — visitors see a small selection of places (name, category and an approximate position), without the name of who added them.
  • Links to other services — buttons such as “Open in Google Maps”, “Directions” or Instagram take you to those services, whose own privacy policies apply.
  • Stored on your device — the site remembers in your browser your language, your photo selection, your login session, a draft of what you are adding and a few display choices (for example notices you have already seen). These are technical settings, not tracking, and you can clear them at any time from your browser.

If you join Spots

  • Account — first name, last name, email and password (stored only in encrypted form), or your name and email from Google if you sign in with Google. Used to create your account, let you log in and approve your membership.
  • What you add — places, photos, reviews, prices you paid, alerts, meetups, the meetups you join, your saved places, flags you send, articles you submit to the Journal and, if you create one, your photographer or local expert profile (name, username, photo, Instagram, website, short bio and an approximate area, rounded to about 5 km). Your name appears next to what you share and is visible to other members only. Saved places, the meetups you join and your activity history are visible only to you.
  • Places from a photo — if you add a place from a photo, its GPS position is read in your browser; the photo is uploaded only if you choose to add it.
  • Trips — the title, the stops (map positions and their names), how you travelled between them, optional dates and your story. To name a stop, its coordinates are sent to Photon (Komoot). Trips are visible to members only, until you delete them. Don't add your home address or where you are right now if you don't want members to know it.
  • Pass it on — the item, its description, whether you sell or give it away, the price, the place and point on the map, the contact details you choose to write (for example a phone number, Instagram or email) and how long the notice stays visible. All of this, contact included, is visible to members only. Notices disappear from the map when they expire and are deleted from the database 7 days later; you can delete yours at any time. Write only your own contact details, and only those you are happy for members to see. What you then exchange privately with other members (messages, phone calls, meetings, payments) happens outside the site: I don't see it, store it or control it.
  • Moderation and admin — I can see flagged content and the requests to join, and I can export the places in an area as a spreadsheet to check and back them up; these exports stay on my own devices. Local experts can verify and edit places in their area.
  • Notifications — when someone signs up, their name and email are sent to me as a phone notification through the ntfy.sh service, so I can approve the request.
  • Where it is kept — in a Supabase database (Supabase Inc.), which acts as processor. Some providers may process data outside the EU under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Legal basis — providing the membership and the features you asked for (GDPR art. 6.1.b) and my legitimate interest in keeping the community safe and moderated and in handling complaints or legal requests (art. 6.1.f).
  • How long — until you delete your content or ask me to close your account. Meetups disappear on their date and Pass it on notices when they expire, and both are removed in periodic clean-ups. Flags and moderation decisions are kept as long as needed to handle them and any related complaint.
  • Disclosure — I share data only with the providers listed here, or with the authorities when the law requires it.
  • Age — Spots is for people aged 16 or over; selling items in Pass it on is for people aged 18 or over.

If you write to me

Emails and Instagram messages (for example about licensing or prints) are used only to answer you and, if we agree on something, to manage that order.

Feedback and contributor requests

The feedback survey is anonymous unless you add your email. If you apply to become a contributor, your name, email, links and message are stored (in Supabase) only to reply to you and evaluate the collaboration; ask me any time to delete them.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or object to its use, by writing to fabio.costantini25491@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.
