How it works

Fabio Costa Ph is three things: a portfolio of my photographs, free tools to plan the light, and SPOTS — a members' map of places worth the trip. Here's how to get the most out of it.

ExploreGalleryJournal ToolsSpotsApp & languages
01

Explore

An interactive map of my journeys. Every astronaut is a place I've photographed.

  • Tap an astronaut to open the place: photos, notes and the story behind it.
  • Use the search (magnifier, top right) to find places, photos and articles.
02

Gallery

All my photographs, filterable by category.

  • Tap a photo to open it full screen. On a phone, turn it sideways or use the rotate button.
  • Tap the heart on the photos you like to build a selection, then send it to me in one message.
  • Every photo is available for licensing (editorial and commercial) and as a fine-art print.
03

Journal

Practical articles, not poetry: when to go, where to park, how long the walk is, what settings I used and the mistakes to avoid.

04

Tools

Free tools to plan a shoot, wherever you are or wherever you're going.

  • Sun & Moon — search a place or tap Near me, pick a date: sunrise and sunset, golden and blue hour, twilight, moon phase and the Milky Way core.
  • Planner — a 3D map with the real terrain: see where the sun, the moon and the Milky Way rise and set behind the mountains. Drag to rotate, move the time and follow them across the sky.
  • Analyze your shot — load a test shot and get a shooting plan: exposure, maximum shutter speed for sharp stars, bracketing and stacking. The photo never leaves your device.
05

Spots

A satellite map of 7,500+ photo spots and points of interest, plus places to eat and sleep, workshops and trails shared by the community.

Visitors and members

  • Everyone sees a free preview: one spot every ~15 km, in an approximate position.
  • Members see every spot with its exact location, photos, reviews, real prices and live alerts — and can add their own.
  • Joining is free: go to fabiocostaph.com/#join, enter your name, email and a password. I approve every request personally, usually within a day.

Finding places

  • Search a spot or a town, or tap Near me and choose the radius (1–50 km).
  • Scan this area adds what OpenStreetMap knows around you: viewpoints, huts, parking, water, protected areas.
  • Filter by category, show or hide places with alerts, or only Support local.

Adding and updating

  • Add a place: only category, name and coordinates are required. Photos, description and community notes are optional.
  • Workshops: add dates, places in total, booked so far, price, a link and any special offer. All upcoming workshops are listed under the map. Update the bookings any time from the workshop's card. It disappears from the map on the day it starts.
  • Trekking: put the pin on the starting point of the trail, so treks are easy to tell apart from viewpoints. Add difficulty (T, E, EE, EEA), time, length, elevation gain and where to park: the average slope is worked out for you.
  • On any place you can add photos, write a review (with what you paid for food and stays) or report a problem.

Categories

  • Photo spots
  • Food & drink
  • Places to stay
  • Workshops
  • Trekking

Signs on the map

  • Red dot — live alert from members: closed, works or landslide, event, very crowded, danger.
  • Green ring — Support local: villages and small businesses that live on visitors.
  • Glowing leaf — new in Support local (first month).
  • Glowing icon — new place or workshop (first 7 days). New and special places stay visible even when you zoom out.
  • %Glowing with % — workshop with a special offer.
  • Plane — workshop almost full: fewer than 3 places left.
  • Dashed border — Fragile place: leave no trace, no geotags.

Many spots are places I've saved but not visited myself: always check access, rules and conditions before you go. Read the full notes below the map.

Privacy

What personal data this site uses, why, and what you can do about it. In short: no advertising, no tracking cookies, nothing sold to anyone.

Who is responsible

The controller of your data is Fabio Costantini (Fabio Costa Ph), Italy. Contact: fabio.costantini25491@gmail.com.

If you just visit the site

  • Statistics — visits are counted with Umami, which uses no cookies and stores no personal data: only anonymous totals (pages viewed, country, device type).
  • Hosting and technical services — the site is served by GitHub Pages; fonts come from Google Fonts; maps and satellite images from Esri, NASA and OpenStreetMap; weather from Open-Meteo; place search from Photon (Komoot). To deliver their content these providers receive your IP address, as with any website.
  • Your location — used only when you tap “Near me” or “Use my location”, processed in your browser and never stored by me.
  • Analyze your shot — the photo you load is analysed in your browser and never uploaded.
  • Stored on your device — the site remembers in your browser your language, your photo selection, your login session and a few display choices. These are technical settings, not tracking, and you can clear them at any time from your browser.

If you join Spots

  • Account — first name, last name, email and password (stored only in encrypted form), or your name and email from Google if you sign in with Google. Used to create your account, let you log in and approve your membership.
  • What you add — places, photos, reviews, prices you paid, alerts, meetups, the meetups you join, your saved places, flags you send and, if you create one, your photographer profile (name, Instagram, website, short bio and an approximate area, rounded to about 5 km). Your name appears next to what you share and is visible to other members only; your saved places are visible only to you.
  • Notifications — when someone signs up, their name and email are sent to me as a phone notification through the ntfy.sh service, so I can approve the request.
  • Where it is kept — in a Supabase database (Supabase Inc.), which acts as processor. Some providers may process data outside the EU under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Legal basis — providing the membership you asked for (GDPR art. 6.1.b) and my legitimate interest in keeping the community safe and moderated (art. 6.1.f).
  • How long — until you delete your content or ask me to close your account. Meetups disappear from the map on their date and are removed in periodic clean-ups.
  • Age — Spots is for people aged 16 or over.

If you write to me

Emails and Instagram messages (for example about licensing or prints) are used only to answer you and, if we agree on something, to manage that order.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or object to its use, by writing to fabio.costantini25491@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.
